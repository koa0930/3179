Global Mine Visualisations

Six idioms built with Vega-Lite using public mining datasets. This page combines a choropleth map, ranking bar chart, concentration trend, stacked area, scatter plot, and a continent detail view to show where mines are located, how production has grown, and how output relates to income.

Global Mine Distribution by Country

Countries are shaded by the number of recorded mine sites. Hover to see the country name, site count, and its share of the global total. Use this map to spot regional hotspots and compare how activity clusters across continents.

Top 10 Countries by Mine Sites

Select a mineral to see the leading producers. Bars are sorted from largest to smallest so you can compare dominance and the gap to the runner up at a glance. Use this chart to identify which countries specialise in a chosen mineral and how concentrated leadership is.

Mineral monopoly rate of top 5 countries

The curve shows the cumulative share captured by the top ranked producers. A steeper rise indicates a highly concentrated market; a flatter curve suggests a more even distribution across producers. Switch minerals to compare how concentration differs by commodity.

Growth of Production by Continent

Total production over time, split by continent. The stacking reveals both overall growth and each continent’s contribution to the global total. Hover to see year totals and continent shares useful for spotting long term trends, plateaus, and shifts in regional leadership.

Effect of Mineral Production on Income per Capita

Each bubble represents a country: x-axis is income per capita, y-axis is total mineral production, colour encodes continent. Filter by continent to explore patterns. Look for clusters and outliers do high output countries also have higher incomes, or are there resource rich economies with lower income per capita?

Detail of Continents

A two level view of composition. The top ring summarises shares at the continent level; the lower ring breaks down countries within the selected context. Use the slider and legend to explore how the mix changes and which countries drive each region.

Sources: Kaggle — Mineral Ores Around the World , World Mining Data
